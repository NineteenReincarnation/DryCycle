======================================================================
1. R5 objective
======================================================================

R5 removes internal Desert Batfly integration debt that was acceptable while Tasks 09–13
were developed independently but is no longer acceptable after R1–R4 established formal
events, shared perception, frame ownership and a single flight boundary.

R5 is not R6. It does not perform mass DB_ renames or final directory migration. A bridge
is deleted only after its unique behavior has a direct formal API/owner path. Empty
compatibility shells are forbidden.

remains a tactical modifier and never becomes a locomotion owner.
