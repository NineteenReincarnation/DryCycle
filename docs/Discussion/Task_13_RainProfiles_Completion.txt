HeavyRain + DeathRain Specialty Specification
Task13 V2.1 Addendum / 2026-09-07
============================================================

STATUS
------
Formal Task13 addendum.
This document replaces the old "baseline only" wording of:
- Section 26 HEAVYRAIN BASELINE PROFILE;
- Section 33 DEATHRAIN BASELINE PROFILE.

All other Task13 V2 ownership, locomotion, persistence, Task09/10/11/12,

This addendum does NOT create Task14 and does NOT expand Task13 into a
second travel controller.

============================================================
1. HEAVYRAIN ECOLOGICAL IDENTITY
============================================================

HeavyRain is strong nonlethal covered-space shelter ecology.
It is NOT LightRain multiplied upward and NOT DeathRain without damage.

Player-facing target:
- open-room activity contracts into roofed pockets, platform undersides,
  alcoves, Hive-adjacent cover and other locally sheltered geometry;
- the colony does not instantly disappear into Hive/Burrow;
- covered pockets remain socially alive at short range;
- individual Injury, Nerve, RoostAffinity and local exposure visibly change
  when each bat contracts.

HeavyRain alone has the phase ceiling:
Calm -> Advisory -> Preparation -> Sheltering -> Recovery.

HeavyRain alone MUST NOT enter Acute.
Acute rain survival belongs to DeathRain.

============================================================
2. HEAVYRAIN SOURCE AND INTENSITY
============================================================

Only validated DryCycle HEAVYRAIN is ecological input.
RoomSettings rain/effects, shader state or visual rain cannot activate it.

Task13 must use actual normalized ActiveIntensity. It must not assume an
active HeavyRain event equals intensity 1.0.

============================================================
3. HEAVYRAIN REALIZED RAIN BURDEN
============================================================

HeavyRain behavior must use a local realized burden rather than intensity
alone.

Required inputs include:
- ActiveIntensity;
- RainExposure;
- RoofShielding;
- ShelterUrgency;
- PhysicalCapability / Injury;
- PostStunShock;
- Nerve.

RoofShielding is the strongest local geometric relief.
Injury/Shock lowers willingness to remain exposed.
Low Nerve contracts earlier.

The burden is realized-only and must never become persistent RainMemory or
weather Trauma.

============================================================
4. HEAVYRAIN SHELTER GEOMETRY
============================================================

Dominant geometry priority:
1) RoofShielding / low RainExposure;
2) Enclosure;
3) reasonable local travel distance;
4) Roost compatibility;
5) crowding;
6) Home/Hive affinity where useful.

A roofed platform underside with open sides can be excellent HeavyRain
shelter.
Side walls with fully open sky are substantially worse.
A legal Roost that remains directly rain-exposed is not automatically a good
environmental Roost.

Shelter remains separate from Roost.
Task10 remains owner of Chain/Roost legality and reservations.

============================================================
5. HEAVYRAIN LOCAL ACTIVITY
============================================================

HeavyRain changes where normal Fly/BatFlight operates, not how physics works.

Allowed:
- localGoal toward a covered shelter anchor;
- shorter activity radius;
- short movement within/between nearby cover pockets;
- sheltered Roost preference;
- moderate same-room Home/Hive pressure when local cover is poor.

Forbidden:
- fixed Y weather height;
- direct BodyChunk.vel writes;
- downward force controller;
- teleport-to-shelter;
- forced universal Burrow;
- HeavyRain-owned LeaveRoom or route planning.

============================================================
6. HEAVYRAIN HOME / BURROW
============================================================

Nearby high-quality covered shelter should normally beat a blanket Hive
recall.

HomeReturnDrive rises mainly when:
- realized rain burden is high;
- nearby cover is poor;
- individual is injured/cautious;
- Home/Hive is already a reasonable local option.

BurrowDrive is a late fallback, not the default HeavyRain answer.
Ordinary HeavyRain must not clear the room merely because a Hive exists.

============================================================
7. HEAVYRAIN INJURY AND DEHYDRATION
============================================================

Injury:
- earlier contraction;
- shorter desired travel to cover;
- stronger Roost/Home preference;
- stronger penalty for exposed long movement;
- slower Recovery.

Task13 must not invent rain-caused wing damage.

Dehydration:
- during Advisory/Preparation and genuinely exposed moderate HeavyRain,
  dehydrated bats may receive weak moisture relief;
- this is weaker than LightRain relief;
- once HeavyRain becomes shelter-dominant, survival pressure wins and the
  bat must not remain exposed merely to gain moisture;
- no drinking Activity and no player thirst-system dependency.

============================================================
8. HEAVYRAIN SOCIAL / HARASS
============================================================

Advisory:
- near-normal local social behavior.

Preparation:
- Companion/Group may continue locally;
- long SocialChase decreases strongly;
- Roost preference increases.

Sheltering:
- short local companion/position negotiation inside cover remains valid;
- long GroupDrift and SocialChase become rare/near-zero;
- shelter pockets should look inhabited, not frozen.

Harass:
- proactive long-range Harass falls as rain burden rises;
- close defensive reactions and immediate danger remain valid;
- HeavyRain never grants DamageAttackPermission.

============================================================
9. HEAVYRAIN + OTHER SYSTEMS
============================================================

Fog combination:
- HeavyRain owns RainExposure/shelter;
- Fog owns VisibilityConfidence/navigation uncertainty;
- HeavyRain itself must leave visibility confidence at 1 and navigation
  uncertainty at 0.

LightRain family overlap:
- HeavyRain dominates rain shelter pressure;
- compatible moisture must not be double-counted.

Task09:
- Task13 can only provide sustained LocalShelterFailure evidence;
- Task09 alone chooses cross-room refuge/route.

HeavyRain -> DeathRain:
- direct escalation into DeathRain Preparation/Sheltering/Acute as applicable;
- no fake intermediate Recovery.

============================================================
10. HEAVYRAIN RECOVERY
============================================================

Initial room recovery baseline remains approximately 150-450 ticks.
Release is individually staggered by Personality/Injury/stable EntityID.
No same-frame colony-wide release from Roost/Hive/shelter pockets.

============================================================
11. DEATHRAIN ECOLOGICAL IDENTITY
============================================================

DeathRain is hard lethal-rain survival.
Its defining ownership split is:

Task09:
- pre-onset cross-room refuge and route safety;
- whether travel remains survivable;
- no unsafe late long-distance movement.

Task13:
- local contraction while still in the current realized room;
- same-room Home/Hive pressure;
- Burrow pressure;
- local covered shelter / long Roost;
- Acute hard survival after the current room is the accepted survival room.

Task13 must never choose the destination room.

============================================================
12. DEATHRAIN PHASE MODEL
============================================================

Use only bounded DryCycle forecast.
Initial tuning constants:
- Preparation lead horizon: about 3600 ticks;
- strong local Sheltering lead horizon: about 1800 ticks.

Expected progression:
- bounded forecast -> Advisory/Preparation;
- near onset or material active rain -> Sheltering;
- material lethal active danger -> Acute.

Acute must require real lethal danger, not merely a forecast token.

============================================================
13. DEATHRAIN LOCAL SURVIVAL DRIVES
============================================================

Unlike old baseline implementation, DeathRain MUST provide actual:
- HomeReturnDrive;
- BurrowDrive;
- ShelterDrive;
- OpenExposureAversion;
- long Roost pressure.

In Sheltering/Acute:
- hard survival suppresses neutral SocialLife and proactive Harass;
- existing attacks are cancelled/shortened according to normal priority;
- Task13 native SurvivalBridge may use existing FlyAI Dijkstra to same-room
  Hive and existing Burrow behavior;
- exposed hard-survival individuals receive especially strong Home/Burrow
  pressure.

This fixes the invalid state where HardSurvival was true but Home/Burrow
pressures were zero.

============================================================
14. DEATHRAIN TASK09 OWNERSHIP
============================================================

If Task09 owns realized travel for the frame:
- Task13 may update context/debug bookkeeping;
- Task13 must restore/preserve Task09 localGoal and Dijkstra ownership;
- Task13 must not pull toward a local shelter anchor.

Pre-onset local contraction is not permission for Task13 to LeaveRoom.

============================================================
15. DEATHRAIN COMBAT / VENGEANCE / SOCIAL
============================================================

DeathRain grants no DamageAttackPermission.

During hard survival:
- neutral play is effectively zero;
- proactive Harass is zero/near-zero;
- Vengeance may be suspended but never cleared by weather;
- immediate physical escape still functions;
- direct survival behavior outranks ordinary Personality aggression.

When recovery completes, pre-existing Vengeance may resume if still valid.

============================================================
16. RAIN ACCEPTANCE SCENARIOS
============================================================

HR-A Open room:
- colony contracts toward several roofed pockets;
- no universal Burrow and no single-point pile.

HR-B Large roof:
- HeavyRain impact is much weaker under broad cover;
- local social life continues.

HR-C Mixed shelter quality:
- several anchors used;
- crowding pushes later bats toward second-best viable cover.

HR-D Injury mix:
- injured/shocked bats contract earlier and travel shorter distances.

HR-E Dehydration mix:
- moderate exposed rain may provide weak moisture;
- strong sheltering pressure still wins.

HR-F HeavyRain + Fog:
- rain shelter and visual confidence remain orthogonal.

HR-G HeavyRain -> DeathRain:
- direct escalation; no Recovery pulse between profiles.

HR-H 20-30 bats:
- no per-frame terrain scan or N^2 weather scan spike.

DR-A forecast:
- visible local contraction begins before lethal onset while Task09 may own
  cross-room refuge.

DR-B near onset:
- local Home/Roost/Burrow pressure becomes strong.

DR-C Acute exposed room:
- hard local survival dominates; no proactive Harass/play.

DR-D Home/Hive present:
- native FlyAI Home/Burrow path is actually used when appropriate.

DR-E Task09 overlap:
- Task09-owned localGoal survives untouched.

DR-F Vengeance overlap:
- weather suspends movement but does not delete Vengeance state.

DR-G recovery:
- individuals leave shelter gradually, not synchronously.

============================================================
17. AUTOMATIC REGRESSION ADDITIONS
============================================================

The Task13 rain regression suite must verify at minimum:
1) HeavyRain strong intensity reaches Sheltering.
2) HeavyRain alone never reaches Acute.
3) HeavyRain exposes realized HeavyRainBurden.
4) Roof shielding materially reduces HeavyRain burden.
5) Injury/Shock increases HeavyRain burden under equal exposure.
6) HeavyRain does not modify Fog visibility/navigation semantics.
7) HeavyRain behavior consumes HeavyRainBurden rather than intensity only.
8) DeathRain preparation lead > shelter lead > 0.
9) DeathRain bounded forecast can enter Preparation.
10) near-onset DeathRain can enter Sheltering.
11) materially lethal DeathRain can enter Acute.
12) native Home/Burrow SurvivalBridge remains present.
13) HeavyRain + LightRain moisture cannot double-count through the secondary bridge.
14) existing Task13 forbidden ownership/velocity/persistence guards remain green.
