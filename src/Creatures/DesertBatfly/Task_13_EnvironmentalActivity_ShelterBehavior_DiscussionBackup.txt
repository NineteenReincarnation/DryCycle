Desert Batfly Task 13 - Discussion Backup
Environmental Activity & Shelter Behavior / 环境活动与房内避险生态
Backup date: 2026-09-07
============================================================

Purpose
-------
This file preserves the current Task13 design discussion before and during weather-by-weather design.
It is a discussion backup. The executable specification is kept separately in Task_13_EnvironmentalActivity_ShelterBehavior.txt.

1. Reframed task role
---------------------
Task13 is no longer a generic "Environmental Group Behavior" controller.
Its role is the realized-room environmental activity and shelter behavior layer.

Task09 answers:
- Can this room still be inhabited safely?
- Should the bat leave the room?
- Which Refuge / Home / route should be used?
- When should ReturnHome happen?

Task13 answers:
- If the bat is staying in this realized room, how should its local activity change?
- Should ordinary flight, harassment, social play, roosting and shelter seeking be suppressed or encouraged?
- Which local covered pocket / shelter anchor should it prefer?
- How should behavior recover after weather ends?

Hard boundary:
- Task13 must never own cross-room route planning, LeaveRoom, HomeColony change, Refuge selection, ColonyMigration or ReturnHome.
- Task09 remains the sole owner of cross-room survival travel.

2. Environmental phase model
----------------------------
Per-room environmental phase proposal:
- Calm
- Advisory
- Preparation
- Sheltering
- Acute
- Recovery

This is environmental context, not a new DesertBatflyAI Activity/Role.

Calm:
- Task13 mostly inactive.
- Task10, Harass, normal Roost, Task11 and vanilla Fly behavior remain normal.

Advisory:
- Near-term weather is observable/forecast but not yet severe.
- Only weak behavior bias.
- High RoostAffinity, low Nerve, injured and high Conformity individuals may begin reducing exposure first.

Preparation:
- Clear ecological activity contraction.
- Harass and SocialChase strongly reduced except for weather profiles that explicitly increase agitation, such as Heat.
- Long solo roaming reduced.
- RoostDrive and local shelter seeking increased.
- Population response must remain staggered by personality/condition.

Sheltering:
- New ordinary Harass is normally heavily suppressed, except where a weather profile explicitly grants heat-aggression permission before hard survival wins.
- Entertainment/neutral Task10 interactions become rare.
- Roost/Chain becomes a major behavior where legal.
- Non-roosting bats stay within local covered pockets rather than forcing illegal Hang points.

Acute:
- Hard survival behavior.
- Personality may no longer justify dangerous ordinary activity.
- Task09 owns control if active cross-room travel is required.
- If the current room is accepted as Home/Refuge, Task13 owns hard local sheltering.

Recovery:
- Weather ending must not release the whole colony on the same frame.
- High Nerve / Temperament may resume first.
- Low Nerve, Trauma and Injury resume later.
- Task10 and ordinary activity gradually return.

3. No fixed flight-height controller
------------------------------------
The old idea "bad weather -> fly lower" is rejected as the core implementation.
Reason:
- It risks recreating vertical correction/oscillation.
- It has weak physical meaning for sandstorm/heat and does not represent actual shelter.

Task13 instead uses Exposure Avoidance:
- reduce open-space exposure;
- prefer roof/wall shielding;
- prefer shaded / enclosed / lee-side / Hive-like areas depending on hazard;
- use localGoal / vanilla Fly locomotion only;
- never directly control BodyChunk.vel.

4. Shelter Anchors
------------------
Current Task09 Refuge terrain analysis can find room shelter quality / one best tile.
Task13 should extend realized-room shelter representation into a small Anchor Set.

Proposal:
- cache approximately 4-8 spatially separated high-quality local shelter anchors per realized room;
- each anchor has ShelterQuality, hazard-specific suitability and current crowding;
- avoid all bats converging on one Vector2;
- anchor choice uses quality, distance, crowding, roost compatibility and stable individual preference;
- anchors are room-runtime data, not persistent identity/roles.

Shelter != Roost:
- Shelter means low environmental exposure.
- Roost means legal Chain/Hang anchor.
- A high-quality shelter can have no legal Roost: bat remains in short-range sheltered flight.
- A legal Roost can be environmentally exposed and therefore be a poor bad-weather choice.
- Roost legality remains Task10 / FlyAI.ChainTile / platform underside rules.

5. Integration with Task10 SocialLife
-------------------------------------
Task13 should provide modifiers rather than duplicate social behavior:
- SocialActivityScale
- HarassScale
- PlayScale
- RoostDriveScale
- GroupCohesionScale
- LocalActivityRadiusMultiplier

General qualitative progression:
Calm: normal.
Advisory: slight weather-specific bias.
Preparation: ordinary non-survival behavior contracts unless the active weather profile explicitly raises agitation.
Sheltering: shelter/roost dominates.
Acute: survival dominates.
Recovery: gradual restoration.

GroupDrift should not be the first neutral behavior disabled; weak group cohesion while moving toward local shelter can look natural, but Task10 must not become a navigation controller.

6. Integration with Task11 Threat Signature
-------------------------------------------
Task11 remains player-threat memory/current cue.
Task13 must not alter or erase ThreatMemory.
Environmental pressure only changes current willingness to spend time on attack/harass behavior.
Real projectile / immediate player danger still receives immediate reaction.

Heat is a special case:
- HeatAgitation may raise attack willingness.
- Task11 still determines how cautiously an experienced individual approaches a dangerous player.
- High Piercing / CounterKill memory can convert heat aggression into more FakeDive, lateral approach, shorter attack windows, etc., rather than canceling heat aggression outright.

7. Integration with Task12 Social Signals
-----------------------------------------
Task12 V1 remains exactly six signals.
Do not add ShelterCall / SandstormCall / WeatherCall in Task13 V1.

Weather is an environmental cue every bat can sense independently; survival may not depend on another bat transmitting it.
Natural coordination can happen through existing RoostCall:
- early responder enters/chooses Roost;
- RoostCall becomes visible;
- high Conformity / Bonded bats may follow via Task10.

AlarmFlutter is not a weather forecast signal.
Only true acute environmental danger may justify an anonymous Alarm hazard origin.
SafeSignal is not the weather-recovery mechanism; Task13 Recovery owns environmental recovery.

8. Personality / condition effects
----------------------------------
Personality changes timing and degree, not whether lethal weather is respected.

Nerve high:
- later Preparation;
- earlier Recovery;
- may remain one of the active individuals during heat agitation.

Nerve low:
- earlier contraction;
- later Recovery.

Conformity high:
- more responsive to Task12 RoostCall / visible neighbor behavior;
- should not copy another bat's Task13 phase directly.

RoostAffinity high:
- earlier Roost commitment when shelter need rises.

Temperament high:
- may continue ordinary activity slightly longer;
- may resume sooner;
- in Heat/IntenseHeat, converts HeatAgitation into attack willingness more strongly.

HardSafetyThreshold:
- once crossed, personality cannot justify continued ordinary harassment/play.

Injury:
- low PhysicalCapability / wing injury / PostStunShock enters preparation sooner;
- prefers nearer anchors;
- crosses less open space;
- roosts sooner;
- recovers later;
- in IntenseHeat, most injured individuals should fail damage-aggression permission and choose survival.
- Task13 does not duplicate InjuryRecovery.

Dehydration:
- increases thermal shelter/exhaustion sensitivity;
- shortens the period in which HeatAgitation can dominate behavior;
- LightRain may provide a weak moisture benefit when the individual is actually exposed to rain.

SocialBond:
- no direct weather-state copying;
- existing RoostCall/Task10/Bond interactions already provide enough social following.

9. Environmental commitment and hysteresis
------------------------------------------
Room phase requires hysteresis, e.g. Preparation enter > exit threshold, Sheltering enter > exit threshold.
Individual shelter commitment is realized-only and should hold for a bounded period (roughly 120-300 ticks) to avoid Calm/Preparation and shelter-target oscillation.
Immediate Danger / Task09 / Grab / Death can interrupt commitment.

10. EnvironmentalInfluence proposal
-----------------------------------
Task13 may expose a realized-only influence struct containing approximately:
- Phase
- ShelterDrive
- OpenExposureAversion
- RoostMultiplier
- HarassMultiplier
- SocialMultiplier
- PlayMultiplier
- GroupCohesionMultiplier
- LocalActivityRadiusMultiplier
- VisibilityConfidence
- NavigationUncertainty
- PreferredShelterPoint
- PreferredShelterQuality
- RecoveryProgress
- HardSurvival
- HeatAgitation
- HeatShelterDrive
- ThermalExhaustion

- BurrowDrive

Explicitly forbidden:

11. Priority proposal
---------------------
Death / unconscious / grabbed
-> Immediate physical danger
-> Severe Injury
-> Task09 active Travel / Emergency Refuge
-> Task13 Acute environmental survival
-> PTSD / Threat avoidance
-> Extreme Vengeance
-> Task13 Preparation / Sheltering / weather-specific agitation modifiers
-> Formal Harass / Retaliation
-> Task12 signal influence
-> Task10 neutral SocialLife
-> ordinary Roost / Swarm / Idle

Extreme Vengeance:
- severe environmental survival may suspend it;
- never clear the vengeance state solely because weather became dangerous;
- allow resumption if the original Vengeance state is still valid after Recovery.
- HeatWave may mildly strengthen active vengeance before hard thermal survival wins.

Retaliation:
- immediate defensive escape remains valid;
- high environmental pressure should shorten long retaliation commitment.

12. No new long-term weather memory
-----------------------------------
Do not add RainTrauma / HeatWaveMemory / SandstormMemory in Task13.
Long-term habitat/weather history remains Task09 MigrationStress / shelter failure / colony history.
Task13 is realized-room behavior.

13. Narrow feedback to Task09
-----------------------------
Task13 may report one bounded ecological fact: LocalShelterFailure.
Example:
- Task09 theoretically rates Home as adequate;
- realized Task13 repeatedly finds no usable anchors / persistent exposure / unusable overcrowding during serious weather;
- after enough duration/evidence, report shelter-failure evidence to Task09.

Task13 must not directly trigger permanent migration.

14. Crowding
------------
Shelter anchor score includes CrowdingPenalty.
Bats should distribute across multiple protected pockets instead of stacking at one point.

15. Local exposure abstraction
------------------------------
Prefer a common local exposure concept/provider rather than hardcoding every weather into one monolithic AI branch.
Conceptual provider data:
- ExposureAt(room, position)
- ShelterPreference / hazard-specific shielding
- optional HazardDirection
- RainExposureAt(position)
- VisibilityAt(position)

Initial fallback can use terrain roof/wall shielding.
Future HeatWave local heat fields, HeatColumn, sunlight or Sandstorm wind direction can extend this without rebuilding the behavior layer.

16. HARD RULE - weather source authority
----------------------------------------
Only DryCycle's own weather runtime/scheduler/spatially-authorized weather event may affect Task13.

Allowed ecological source:
- DryCycle WeatherScheduleRuntime / scheduled weather candidate;
- DryCycle WeatherSpatialRegistry authorization for the actual room;
- DryCycle active/forecast Weather or DangerType event;
- future DryCycle local exposure provider explicitly connected to that active event.

Forbidden ecological source:
- vanilla/default RoomSettings Fog effect;
- RoomSettings visual Fog amount;
- palette/effect values that only make a room look foggy;
- an arbitrary vanilla Effect or modded visual effect that is not the active DryCycle weather event;
- inferring weather from shader/graphics state.

Example hard test:
RoomSettings Fog = strong, DryCycle weather = None -> Task13 must remain Calm and visibility/activity must remain unaffected.
DryCycle DenseFog active -> Task13 DenseFog behavior may run even if the visual implementation internally uses RoomSettings/Fog rendering.

17. LightRain - weak disturbance / moisture opportunity
-------------------------------------------------------
Ecological identity:
- LightRain is not a miniature HeavyRain.
- It is basically safe, mildly disruptive and potentially slightly beneficial in a desert ecology.

Phase ceiling:
- LightRain by itself may reach only weak Advisory.
- It must never independently force Preparation/Sheltering/Acute or Task09 evacuation.

Healthy individuals:
- mostly continue normal Flight, CompanionDrift, GroupDrift, PassBy, SocialChase, Harass and ordinary Roost.
- no meaningful shelter-cluster formation.

Sensitive individuals:
- low Nerve, high RoostAffinity, Injury/PostStunShock may slightly reduce activity radius and roost earlier.

Moisture benefit:
- dehydrated individuals may tolerate LightRain better and may remain in exposed rain longer.
- only true local rain exposure counts; roof-protected individuals do not receive the same benefit.
- use simple dehydration recovery, not a new drinking animation or integration with the player's full thirst system.

Task10:
- Companion/Group/PassBy essentially normal.
- SocialChase only slightly reduced.
- RoostInvitation/ChainSocialization slightly increased.

Harass:
- only a tiny reduction for ordinary healthy individuals.
- high Temperament/high Nerve healthy bats can behave essentially normally.

Signals:
- never emit AlarmFlutter merely because LightRain is active.
- do not use SafeSignal when LightRain ends.

Recovery:
- short and staggered; only individuals that actually reduced activity need a visible recovery delay.

Task09:
- no permanent migration pressure from LightRain.
- no EmergencyRefuge solely because of LightRain.

18. Fog - reduced visual confidence / reduced activity
------------------------------------------------------
Ecological identity:
- Fog is a sensory/navigation condition, not primarily a shelter hazard.
- The main effects are lower visibility confidence, smaller activity radius and lower willingness for long-range activity.

Core effects:
- VisibilityConfidence moderately reduced.
- Solo roaming radius reduced.
- long-distance Harass/SocialChase reduced.
- nearby CompanionDrift/PassBy/small GroupDrift can remain.
- ordinary Roost may increase slightly, but Fog is not a shelter emergency.

System integration:
- Task10 visual partner/signal/target search range may be reduced.
- Task11 long-range CurrentCue/witness recognition may be reduced, but a projectile already close enough to be immediate danger must remain detectable.
- Task12 visual signals (Rally/Roost/Harass/Safe) get shorter effective range; Alarm/Distress retain their close acoustic/flutter channel.

Fog must not create new long-term weather memory.
Fog must not trigger permanent migration or normal EmergencyRefuge by itself.

19. DenseFog - severe visual uncertainty / temporary displacement
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Ecological identity:
- DenseFog is a qualitative step beyond ordinary Fog.
- Normal life becomes difficult because navigation and visual coordination are unreliable.

Core effects:
- VisibilityConfidence strongly reduced.
- ActivityRadius strongly reduced.
- Harass and long-distance SocialLife strongly reduced.
- Roost/Home-return desire increases.
- injured/low-Nerve individuals withdraw earlier.

Navigation uncertainty:
- obstacle anticipation distance is reduced rather than forcing random collision.
- estimated localGoal/shelter target may contain bounded positional uncertainty at long range.
- uncertainty shrinks as the bat approaches the target.
- occasional real wall collision may naturally result because avoidance starts late.
- never force collisions by setting velocity into terrain.
- no fixed collision timer/probability independent of geometry.

Familiarity:
- Home/Hive room familiarity can reduce uncertainty/error.
- known Roost/Shelter areas may be easier to approach than unfamiliar room geometry.

Task09 handoff:
- DenseFog may justify temporary Weather Refuge / displacement if the current room becomes unusable and a reachable clearer safe room exists.
- this is temporary EmergencyRefuge/ReturnHome behavior, NOT permanent ColonyMigration.
- if Home is familiar and locally adequate, remaining/returning Home may be preferred over outward displacement.

Recovery:
- visibility/activity confidence returns gradually after DenseFog clears.

20. HeatWave - simultaneous agitation and heat avoidance
-------------------------------------------------------
Heat cannot be represented by one monotonic "activity down" value.
It has at least three simultaneous derived drives:
- HeatAgitation: thermal agitation / increased aggression.
- HeatShelterDrive: desire for shade, Roost, Hive or other heat shelter.
- ThermalExhaustion: accumulated cost of prolonged exposure.

Typical progression:
Early HeatWave:
- HeatAgitation rises faster than exhaustion.
- Harass, FakeDive, Dive willingness and general agitation increase.
- some SocialChase may increase briefly.

Middle HeatWave:
- agitation remains high;
- HeatShelterDrive and ThermalExhaustion grow;
- group splits: some remain aggressive, others start Roost/shade/Hive behavior.

Long/severe HeatWave:
- shelter/exhaustion increasingly wins;
- long-distance flight and neutral play fall;
- more individuals choose shaded Roost/local shelter/Home proximity.

Aggression:
- ordinary HeatWave increases damage-capable attack permission mainly for already bold/aggressive/healthy individuals and some medium individuals.
- it must not turn every low-Nerve individual into a damage attacker.
- AttackSlots, PTSD, Injury, Task11 caution and immediate survival gates remain.

Shelter geometry:
- Heat seeks shade/roof/local thermal relief, not arbitrary low altitude.
- a legal Roost in direct hot exposure may score worse than a shaded non-roost shelter pocket.
- future HeatColumn/local heat field API should plug into local ExposureAt.

Dehydration:
- high dehydration increases HeatShelterDrive and ThermalExhaustion.
- high dehydration shortens the period in which HeatAgitation can dominate.

Task10:
- early heat can mildly increase SocialChase/active agitation.
- later heat suppresses neutral play while RoostInvitation/ChainSocialization increase.

Task11:
- learned player danger changes attack geometry/timing, not the existence of heat agitation.

Vengeance:
- may be mildly strengthened while thermal survival remains below hard threshold.
- hard thermal survival suspends, but never clears, Vengeance.

21. IntenseHeat / Danger Heat - extreme aggression permission plus hard survival
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ecological identity:
- DangerType INTENSEHEAT is both extremely agitating and extremely dangerous.
- it must not be implemented as pure berserk mode or pure hiding mode.

Core drives:
- HeatAgitation = very high.
- HeatShelterDrive = very high.
- ThermalExhaustion grows rapidly.

Damage aggression permission:
- a majority of healthy adult individuals may become eligible for genuinely damaging attacks.
- eligibility is not the same as everyone attacking simultaneously.
- AttackSlots, distance, target opportunity, current shelter state and AI gates still limit concurrency.
- severe Injury, unconsciousness, Task09 Travel, hard shelter commitment and severe PTSD can veto damage aggression.

Population split goal:
- many individuals return Home/Hive, long-Roost or Burrow under extreme heat;
- individuals that remain active tend to be high Nerve/high Temperament/healthy and therefore the visible active population can become more dangerous even as total active population falls.

Long Roost:
- thermal Roost commitment may last far longer than ordinary RoostDuration, potentially through the high-intensity portion of the event.
- real danger/grab/Task09 still interrupts.

Burrow:
- existing Burrow/Emergence system should be reused.
- increase Burrow preference near legal sand/hive areas; do not add a duplicate HeatBurrow AI mode.

Home return:
- current-room Home/Hive retreat is Task13 local behavior.
- cross-room Home/Refuge travel remains Task09.

Derived HeatTolerance:
- do not add a new persistent personality axis.
- derive from existing Nerve, PhysicalCapability, small Temperament contribution, Injury, PostStunShock, Dehydration and stable EntityID variation.

22. Sandstorm - anticipatory Home Retention
-------------------------------------------
Ecological identity:
- Sandstorm makes outward travel itself dangerous.
- unlike DenseFog, the default answer is not "find a clearer room" but "get Home / stay Home / Burrow / Roost / shelter locally".

Core drives:
- HomeReturnDrive strongly increased.
- BurrowDrive strongly increased.
- RoostDrive strongly increased.
- open-space exposure strongly avoided.
- discretionary cross-room travel strongly suppressed.
- new permanent ColonyMigration strongly suppressed during strong preparation/active Sandstorm.

Unique anticipatory ability:
- Desert Batfly has a species-specific early response to approaching Sandstorm.
- use the bounded DryCycle forecast window; do not grant full-cycle scheduler omniscience.
- Sandstorm begins visible behavior contraction significantly earlier than comparable HeavyRain/Fog hazards.

Early anticipation visual goal:
- some high RoostAffinity/low Nerve/injured individuals head Home or roost early;
- far-roaming activity radius shrinks;
- SocialChase and ordinary Harass begin falling;
- later, larger parts of the group return Home/Burrow/Roost.
- experienced players should be able to infer that a Sandstorm is coming from bat behavior.

Shelter geometry:
- Sandstorm shelter values enclosure and side shielding in addition to roof.
- if DryCycle later exposes wind direction, prefer lee-side/upwind-wall shielding.
- until then, use enclosure/roof/nearby wall geometry as fallback.

Attack behavior:
- ordinary proactive Harass decreases overall.
- defensive aggression can increase if Player blocks Hive/Burrow access or attacks a returning bat.

Task12:
- do not add SandstormCall.
- individual weather anticipation plus existing RoostCall produces local social coordination.

Task09:
- strong Sandstorm preparation/active weather should gate NEW permanent migration batches.
- emergency outward travel is allowed only when current Home/location is materially unsafe and a clearly safer, reachable nearby Refuge can be reached before the storm becomes dangerous.
- once serious Sandstorm is active, avoid starting long cross-room travel.

23. DeathSandstorm / Danger Sandstorm - hard retention and survival
------------------------------------------------------------------
Ecological identity:
- same basic strategy as Sandstorm, but much stronger and less permissive.

Before onset:
- unique early Sandstorm anticipation becomes especially important.
- return Home/Burrow/long Roost well before lethal conditions when possible.

After high danger begins:
- long-distance relocation is normally prohibited.
- only a very short, clearly survivable hard-emergency retreat to a materially safer Refuge may override.
- permanent migration must not start.

Priority:
- hard sandstorm survival can suspend Extreme Vengeance/Harass/SocialLife without erasing their long-term state.

24. Fog vs Sandstorm temporary travel distinction
-------------------------------------------------
DenseFog:
- travel itself may still be viable;
- if the current room is unusably fogged and a nearby clearer safe room exists, temporary displacement can be reasonable.

Sandstorm:
- the route outside is itself dangerous;
- bias toward Home retention, Burrow, Hive, enclosed local shelter;
- outward Refuge travel requires stronger justification and should happen before serious onset.

This distinction is mandatory. Task13/Task09 must not collapse all bad weather into "pick nearest refuge".

25. Weather coexistence / dominance principles
----------------------------------------------
- do not blindly add two copies of the same family behavior (e.g. LightRain + HeavyRain).
- select a dominant ecological hazard profile, then allow secondary modifiers only where semantically compatible.
- HeavyRain dominates LightRain shelter behavior.
- LightRain may weakly reduce heat/dehydration burden but never cancel HeatWave/IntenseHeat.
- Fog can coexist with LightRain because one is visibility pressure and the other is weak moisture/rain exposure.
- hard DangerType survival dominates neutral weather modifiers.

26. HeavyRain / DeathRain retained baseline pending deeper weather-specific discussion
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HeavyRain baseline:
- classic roof/covered-space shelter weather;
- Roost and local shelter seeking increase;
- open-sky activity, ordinary Harass and SocialChase decrease;
- Task09 handles cross-room refuge if Home is inadequate.

DeathRain baseline:
- hard survival;
- Task09 decides whether the bat can/should leave before lethal onset;
- once lethal rain is already active, do not start unsafe long-distance travel;
- if staying in an adequate room, Task13 Acute local shelter dominates.

No additional special HeavyRain/DeathRain ecology should be invented until separately discussed.

27. Updated core decisions
--------------------------
1) Task13 owns realized-room environmental behavior only; Task09 owns cross-room behavior.
2) Only DryCycle active/forecast weather events can drive Task13; default RoomSettings/visual effects never count.
3) Exposure/shelter replaces fixed low-flight-height behavior.
4) Six environmental phases: Calm, Advisory, Preparation, Sheltering, Acute, Recovery.
5) Shelter and Roost are separate concepts.
6) Cache multiple Shelter Anchors, not one flock target point.
7) Personality changes timing/strength but not lethal safety rules.
8) Task10 receives modifiers; no duplicated social system.
9) Task12 V1 remains six signals; no new weather signal.
10) Task11 ThreatMemory remains independent and private.
11) Extreme Vengeance pauses under hard environmental survival, but is not erased.
12) Task13 adds no independent long-term weather memory.
13) Task13 may report bounded LocalShelterFailure evidence to Task09, never migration itself.
14) Shelter movement uses localGoal / vanilla Fly locomotion only; no velocity controller.
15) Recovery must be staggered and cannot release the whole colony on one frame.
16) LightRain stays near-normal and can offer a weak moisture benefit.
17) Fog primarily lowers visual confidence/activity; DenseFog may cause temporary weather displacement and navigation mistakes.
18) Heat simultaneously raises aggression and heat-shelter pressure; IntenseHeat allows damage aggression for many healthy individuals while driving many others to Home/Roost/Burrow.
19) Sandstorm uniquely triggers early Home-retention behavior and suppresses outward migration/travel.
20) DeathSandstorm strongly favors pre-onset return and discourages late travel.
